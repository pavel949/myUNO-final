# DESIGN.md — myUNO Experience and Visual Direction

## 1. Thesis

myUNO should feel like a **quiet premium hospitality network with serious operating machinery underneath**.

Not luxury cliché, generic SaaS, marketplace clutter, fintech dashboard or an old property portal.

The current Andaman green / warm ivory / restrained gold palette and typography are broadly compatible. Preserve mature primitives and tokens; materially improve composition, hierarchy and photography.

## 2. Emotional target

- Public: desirable + trustworthy + useful.
- Guest: personal + calm + anticipatory.
- Owner: transparent + controlled + evidence-based.
- Operator: clear + fast + exception-focused.
- Control Plane: quiet command center.

## 3. Visual principles

### Photography is structural
Use large real property/context/service imagery to create rhythm. Avoid generic tropical stock, watermarks, tiny thumbnail walls and decorative imagery without utility.

### Fewer, stronger blocks
Prefer one dominant idea per viewport, strong whitespace and editorial rhythm. Avoid equal-weight card walls, KPI tile overload and feature soup.

### Density follows job
Public = spacious/editorial. Operations = denser/task-focused. Same visual language; different information density.

## 4. Color

Keep deep Andaman, ivory, paper, ink, stone and restrained warm gold. Gold should remain scarce. Avoid rainbow statuses and making every section dark green.

## 5. Typography

Current Outfit + Manrope direction is acceptable. Support Cyrillic and Thai. Public hero: one short title, one short line, one practical search/action.

## 6. Homepage composition

```text
[light/transparent header]
[full-width image hero 72–82vh]
  small proposition
  short headline
  concise supporting line
  floating stay search
  secondary services link

Places on myUNO
  large editorial property cards

Ways to stay / editorial split

Your Phuket, handled
  service categories with real imagery

One trip, one place
  actual Trip Hub product proof

Own a property?
  Manage with myUNO | List/self-manage

Operate properties or provide services?

Trust / standard / responsibility

footer
```

Mobile retains hero imagery; search becomes a compact sheet; cards are near-full-width.

## 7. Navigation

Consumer-first:
`myUNO · Explore · Stays · Services · Homes · For Owners · For Partners · My myUNO`.

Do not expose CRM, PMS, OperatingScope, finance or provider-table language publicly.

## 8. Cards

Use a card only for a naturally selectable object. Property cards are image-dominant with destination, truthful relationship label and authoritative availability/price only. Service cards show service, provider/fulfillment, price mode, lead time and confirmation mode.

## 9. Relationship labels

Quiet but explicit: Managed by myUNO; Residences managed by myUNO; Available through myUNO; Partner managed; Owner managed; Operated to myUNO standard. These are trust metadata, not decorative badges.

## 10. Interaction grammar

Low stakes: one tap/inline/optimistic only where safe. High stakes: explicit review, consequences, confirmation and server acknowledgment. Never show success before canonical confirmation.

## 11. Operational UX

Manager: Today / Needs Attention / cases.  
Frontline: My Work / due / instructions / evidence.  
Owner: My Homes / money / condition / bookings / approvals / history.  
Partner: My Portfolio / delegated capability only.

## 12. States

Every data surface has loading, empty, error, permission denied, stale and partial-data behavior where relevant. Never confuse loading/error with genuine empty state.

## 13. Copy

Calm, specific, useful, restrained. Avoid “revolutionary”, “ultimate”, “luxury redefined”, unsupported 24/7 claims, unsupported ROI or vague ecosystem language.

Prefer factual responsibility copy such as “Selected residences managed by myUNO” or “Availability is confirmed by the property.”

## 14. Design review gate

A screen is incomplete until hierarchy is obvious in five seconds, one primary action is clear, content is not duplicated, mobile is intentional, photography has purpose, responsibility is truthful, all states exist, accessibility basics pass, tokens are respected and real data shapes were tested.

Ask: **Does this feel like one calm coherent myUNO product, or another feature page added by another team?** If the second, redesign.

## 15. Localization acceptance

Review meaning and quality, not just key existence: RU copy, EN consistency, Thai typography/wrapping, long Russian strings, form/errors/notifications. A translation key existing is not proof of translation quality.
