## Brand & Style

### Brand Personality & Philosophy
The design system embodies serene tropical luxury, architectural honesty, and white-glove real estate stewardship rooted in Phuket's coastal geography. Built for affluent global travelers, high-net-worth property investors, and discerning long-term residents, the interface projects calm confidence, institutional reliability, and transparency. It avoids loud commercial gimmicks, synthetic promises, and generic tropical clichés in favor of grounded sophistication: textured mineral surfaces, verdant foliage depth, warm sunlight warmth, and structural precision.

### Aesthetic Movement: Tropical Architectural Modernism
The visual direction fuses **Minimalism** with **Tactile Warmth** and **Organic Architecture**. Rather than sterile tech minimalism or hyper-glossy resort aesthetics, this design language evokes modern pavilion villas—natural travertine stone, teak accents, deep shade overhangs, and lush coastal jungle flora. 

Key visual principles include:
- **Atmospheric Calm**: Generous negative space that allows architectural photography to breathe without feeling hollow.
- **Truth to Materials & Truth to Data**: Visual components reflect verifiable asset metrics. Media strictly separates actual physical unit photography from representative lifestyle views.
- **Uncompromised Usability**: Critical discovery mechanisms—notably the multi-intent booking and acquisition search rail—remain anchored and accessible above the fold across all mobile viewports.

## Colors

### Palette Architecture
The palette is built from the Andaman ecosystem: the deep, shadow-drenched green of rain-soaked jungle canopies, warm mineral ivory sands, and sunlit terracotta-gold stone accents.

- **Primary (`#11382E` - Andaman Emerald)**: The anchor of all authority, branding elements, high-priority actions, and structured navigation. It conveys permanence, prestige, and quiet confidence.
- **Secondary (`#D19A5B` - Terracotta Gold)**: Used judiciously for contextual highlights, featured badges, interactive accents, rating stars, and subtle price emphasis. It introduces sunlight and hospitality warmth without veering into ostentatious luxury yellow-gold.
- **Tertiary (`#0B2C24` - Deep Canopy)**: A darker, near-black emerald utilized for high-contrast headers, deep active states, and grounding structural footer zones.
- **Neutral (`#1C2826` - Dark Slate)**: The text baseline. Far softer and more organic than harsh `#000000`, providing crisp, comfortable reading contrast across long property briefs and contractual breakdowns.

### Surfaces & Intent Badging
- `surface-ivory` (`#FBF9F5`) serves as the global canvas, radiating natural light.
- `surface-sand` (`#F4F0E8`) defines card containers, subtle group backgrounds, and module separators.
- Operational verification badges, scope indicators, and trust evidence employ dedicated high-readability pairings: `status-verified` (`#1E5848`) on `status-verified-bg` (`#E9F1EE`) ensures clarity without competing with primary discovery pathways.

## Typography

### Structural Contrast
The typographic hierarchy couples **Outfit** for structural headings with **Manrope** for analytical readability and interface mechanics.

- **Outfit** carries sculpted geometric letterforms with an architectural presence. Its open apertures and subtle geometric angles capture high-end modern residential styling without decorative frivolity. Display titles use tight letter-spacing (`-0.02em`) to command attention.
- **Manrope** provides pragmatic clarity across property data readouts, pricing matrices, inventory specifications (sqm, bed/bath count, operational scope), and contract terms. Its high x-height and consistent stroke terminals preserve absolute legibility across dense data clusters.

### Sizing and Rhythmic Scaling
The typographic scale responds to viewport boundaries without sacrificing spatial balance:
- Mobile hero headers collapse gracefully from 56px to 36px, strictly preventing title overflow that could push the search bar beneath the initial screen fold.
- Eyebrow badges, operational mandates, and verification stamps consistently utilize `label-caps` in uppercase format with wide tracking (`0.08em`) to balance visual weight against architectural headlines.

## Layout & Spacing

### Layout Model & Viewport Disciplines
The layout operates on a standard **12-column fluid grid** for desktop and tablet, collapsing to a **4-column grid** on mobile viewports. Max container width is fixed at `1280px` to maintain optimal viewing angles for high-resolution residential photography.

- **Desktop (>= 1024px)**: 12 columns, `1.5rem` (24px) gutters, `3rem` (48px) lateral margin.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px)**: 8 columns, `1.25rem` (20px) gutters, `2rem` (32px) lateral margin.
- **Mobile (<= 767px)**: 4 columns, `1rem` (16px) gutters, `1.25rem` (20px) lateral margin.

### The Above-The-Fold Rule
Full-viewport (`100vh`) hero images that hide critical discovery tools are forbidden. The mobile hero section strictly reserves space for:
1. Compact headline & value proposition.
2. The interactive multi-intent selector (`Stay`, `Lease`, `Buy`).
3. Essential search input fields (Location, Dates/Term, Guests).
All primary controls must sit above the mobile viewport boundary.

### Grid Rhythm and Inventory Density
Rhythm enforces calm, unhurried negative space:
- **Complexes & Master Residences**: Rendered in a relaxed 2-column or 3-column grid to accentuate grandeur and grounds.
- **Individual Units**: Rendered in an efficient 3-column or 4-column layout prioritizing price transparency, configuration badges, and booking capability. When inventory is constrained, containers shrink gracefully rather than artfully padding space with duplicate or synthetic cards.

## Elevation & Depth

### Depth Philosophy: Tonal Surfaces over Heavy Drop Shadows
In alignment with tropical architectural elegance, elevation is achieved primarily through warm tonal stratification rather than dark, disembodied drop shadows. The design layers tactile surfaces (`#FBF9F5` base to `#F4F0E8` container to `#FFFFFF` floating element), reminiscent of limestone and light oak layered atop one another.

### Ambient Shadow Hierarchy
Where elevation is physically required to separate floating interaction layers from scrolling inventory:
- **Surface Level 0 (Base Canvas)**: Flat `#FBF9F5`. No shadow.
- **Surface Level 1 (Structural Cards & Modules)**: Flat `#F4F0E8` or `#FFFFFF` bounded by a fine, low-contrast border: `1px solid #E5DFD3`.
- **Surface Level 2 (Interactive Hover & Contextual Cards)**: Very subtle, warm-tinted shadow: `box-shadow: 0 4px 20px -2px rgba(17, 56, 46, 0.05), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(17, 56, 46, 0.02)`.
- **Surface Level 3 (Search Bar Rail & Dropdown Menus)**: Clean, floating elevation with subtle tint: `box-shadow: 0 12px 32px -4px rgba(11, 44, 36, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(11, 44, 36, 0.03)`.
- **Surface Level 4 (Modal Overlays & Inquiries)**: Centered focal depth: `box-shadow: 0 24px 48px -12px rgba(11, 44, 36, 0.16)`. Accompanied by a diffused backdrop wash: `backdrop-filter: blur(8px); background-color: rgba(11, 44, 36, 0.4)`.

### Border Integrity
Crisp, low-opacity borders (`#E5DFD3`) define every card and container, guaranteeing legibility even in sunlit outdoor viewing environments common in destination resort settings.

## Shapes

### Shape Language
The design system employs **Rounded (`2`)** geometry (`0.5rem` / `8px` baseline). This balances natural organic curves with crisp architectural discipline. 

- **Small Interactive Items (Chips, Badges, Tooltips)**: `rounded` (`0.5rem` / `8px`) provides unified, approachable tactile targets.
- **Cards, Modular Panels & Sheets**: `rounded-lg` (`1rem` / `16px`) delivers soft framing that respects photographic aspect ratios without cropping corner details.
- **Search Bars & Floating Action Rails**: `rounded-xl` (`1.5rem` / `24px`) or soft capsule silhouettes, delineating search and discovery mechanisms from the static page structure.
- **Inputs & Form Controls**: `0.5rem` (8px), matching the core button baseline to establish cohesive horizontal scan lines across form fields.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Book, Inquire, Search)**: Solid Andaman emerald (`#11382E`) background with pure white (`#FFFFFF`) text. Min height `48px` (desktop) and `44px` (mobile). Subtle transition to `#0B2C24` on hover. Focus ring: `2px solid #D19A5B` with `2px` offset.
- **Secondary Action (View Residence, Filter)**: Outlined with `1px solid #11382E`, transparent background, `#11382E` text. Fills with `#11382E` at `0.04` opacity on hover.
- **Tertiary / Accent Action (Concierge Request, Instant Confirm)**: Solid terracotta gold (`#D19A5B`) background with white text (`#FFFFFF`) for specific conversion milestones.
- **Motion Rule**: Interactions must be rapid and immediate (150ms ease-out). No transition may delay tap response or block form submission.

### Intent Rail & Search Switcher
- Persistent segmented control facilitating instant switching between **"Stay / Для отдыха"**, **"Long-Term / Надолго"**, and **"Buy / Купить"**.
- State preservation: Changing tabs updates form fields dynamically (e.g., check-in/out dates swap to lease duration months or purchase budget brackets) without resetting previously chosen location parameters.
- Active tab pill: Soft ivory surface (`#FFFFFF`) with subtle shadow over the sand rail background (`#F4F0E8`), with label colored in `#11382E`.

### Cards
- **Complex / Development Card**: Large visual scale. Emphasizes total project grounds, architecture, shared amenities, and active management mandates. Contains persistent badge for operational scope (e.g., "Full Mandate by myUNO").
- **Individual Property Unit Card**: Compact, data-dense, highly scannable footprint:
  - Strict 4:3 or 16:10 photography container.
  - Image carousel indicators overlaid directly on media.
  - Media truth indicator: Small tag indicating "Actual Unit" versus "Representative Complex" view.
  - Configuration string: Bedrooms, bathrooms, internal area in m².
  - Dynamic booking badge: Explicit state indicator (`Instant Booking`, `Request Booking`, or `Private Inquiry`).
  - Clear rate readout with tax/fee transparency note.

### Badges & Trust Evidence Tags
- **Trust Badges**: Compact pills strictly tied to verified operational data:
  - *Managed Unit*: `status-verified` text on `status-verified-bg`.
  - *Instant Confirmation*: `status-instant` text on `status-instant-bg`.
- Generic, decorative, or unverified claims are prohibited.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- Background: `#FFFFFF` or `#FBF9F5` set inside `#F4F0E8` panels.
- Border: `1px solid #E5DFD3`.
- Active / Focused: `1.5px solid #11382E` border with no harsh glow; crisp and modern.
- Typography: Label in `label-caps` (`#566360`) positioned strictly above the input; value in `body-md` (`#1C2826`).
- Date & Guest Pickers: Floating modal cards rendered on Surface Level 3 elevation with generous touch targets (minimum `44x44px` per date cell).

### Checkboxes & Radio Selectors
- Radio buttons feature dual concentric rings using Andaman emerald (`#11382E`).
- Checkboxes use an `8px` rounded square with emerald fill and crisp white checkmark icon upon selection.

### Transparency & Pricing Breakdown Module
- Dedicated disclosure sheet within property view: Itemizes base rate, cleaning/service surcharge, utilities policy (electricity/water per unit meter reading), and refundable security deposit.
- Typography: Strict tabular alignment using Manrope numerical figures.
